PROCESS CHEMISTRY

Quality by Design (QbD) & Route Scouting

Designing cost-effective, non-infringing, robust, and environmentally benign chemical routes that scale seamlessly from grams to commercial metric tons.

📐

Quality by Design (QbD) Principles

Systematic implementation of ICH Q8(R2) Quality by Design guidelines. Defining Critical Quality Attributes (CQAs) and Critical Process Parameters (CPPs) to establish reliable design spaces.

  • Design of Experiments (DoE) statistical factor screening
  • Risk assessments via FMEA methodology
  • Robust parameter ranges preventing batch failures
🛡️

Non-Infringing Route Engineering

Independent patent landscape analysis to scout non-infringing synthetic pathways, bypassing innovator claims and granting our partners early-to-market generic launch advantages.

  • Exhaustive Freedom-to-Operate (FTO) patent clearances
  • Alternate starting material strategies
  • Novel crystalline forms and non-infringing salts

Green Chemistry & Atom Economy

Minimizing the process E-Factor by substituting hazardous solvents with greener alternatives, recycling process streams, and optimizing stoichiometric atom efficiency.

  • Solvent elimination and closed-loop recycling
  • Catalytic processes eliminating stoichiometric reagents
  • Drastic reduction in process effluent volumes

TOXICOLOGY & SAFETY

Impurity Profiling & Nitrosamine Assessment

Auditor-grade characterization of process-related impurities, degradation products, genotoxic impurities (GTIs), and nitrosamines meeting USFDA and EMA thresholds.

🔬

Isolation & Structural Elucidation

Preparative HPLC isolation of unknown impurities above 0.05% threshold, followed by multi-nuclear NMR, LC-MS/MS, and high-resolution MS characterization.

  • Synthesized in-house impurity reference standards
  • Complete spectral characterization dossiers
  • Certificate of Analysis (COA) with assigned purity
⚠️

Nitrosamine Risk Assessment (NRA)

Comprehensive risk assessments conducted across all synthesis routes, raw materials, solvents, and recovered reagents to prevent NDMA, NDEA, and complex NDSRIs.

  • Conforms to USFDA & EMA guidance on nitrosamines
  • LC-MS/MS trace quantification below AI limits
  • Confirmed absence of secondary amine contamination
⚡

Elemental Impurities (ICH Q3D)

Risk-based elemental impurity evaluation covering Class 1, 2A, 2B, and 3 elements via ICP-MS, ensuring compliance with global pharmacopoeial monographs.

  • Class 1 toxic heavy metals (Cd, Pb, As, Hg) verified
  • Transition catalyst clearance (Pd, Ru, Rh, Pt)
  • Documented risk assessment summaries for DMF Module 3
