WHO WE ARE

A Fast-Growing Leader in APIs & Complex Intermediates

Founded in 2008, ADEEP GROUP encompasses Adeep Life Sciences Pvt Ltd and Adeep Laboratories Pvt Ltd. From humble beginnings, we have scaled into an integrated pharmaceutical manufacturing powerhouse.

★ Our Journey & Legacy Est. 2008

17+ Years of Transforming Chemical Engineering into Life-Saving Therapies

ADEEP is a mission-driven innovator committed to India’s pharmaceutical self-reliance. Aligned deeply with national initiatives, we have systematically expanded domestic manufacturing to eliminate critical dependencies on foreign suppliers.

3 High-Capacity Units & Central R&D Scale & Capacity
Continuous multi-ton synthesis facilities in Bidar and Kopparthi (Kadapa), supported by ADEEP Technology Center (Central R&D) in Hyderabad.
Global Regulatory Footprint cGMP & Global Standards

Diversified Product Portfolio 50+ Commercial Products

Green Chemistry & Sustainable Innovation Biocatalysis & ZLD
