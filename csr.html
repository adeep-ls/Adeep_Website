COMMUNITY IMPACT

Core Pillars of ADEEP CSR Initiatives

Guided by Schedule VII of the Indian Companies Act and UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs 3, 4, 6, and 8).

🎓

Youth & Science Education

We actively invest in building India's next generation of pharmaceutical chemists, laboratory technicians, and quality analysts through educational empowerment.

  • ADEEP Chemistry Merit Scholarships: Full tuition grants for promising undergraduate chemistry students.
  • Government Polytechnic Apprenticeships: Hands-on technical stipends and plant exposure for diploma holders.
  • Rural School Infrastructure: Modernized digital classrooms, libraries, and science labs in neighboring villages.
💧

Clean Drinking Water & Health

Securing potable drinking water and preventative primary healthcare to elevate living standards across communities surrounding our manufacturing hubs.

  • Automated RO Water Kiosks: High-capacity reverse osmosis units delivering purified drinking water 24/7.
  • Quarterly Medical Screening Camps: Free eye, cardiac, and general diagnostic camps conducted by specialist doctors.
  • Primary Health Center Aid: Essential life-saving pharmaceutical supplies donated to rural clinics.
👥

Fair Labor & Inclusive Growth

Fostering dignity of labor, equal employment opportunities, and specialized scientific career pathways for women chemists and rural youth.

  • Women in Chemistry Initiative: Active mentorship and leadership roles across QC, QA, and R&D.
  • Strict Zero-Child-Labor Policy: Independent third-party audited employment charters across all facilities.
  • Comprehensive Family Healthcare: Full insurance, subsidized nutrition, and transport provided to all staff.
FLAGSHIP CASE STUDY

Bidar Rural Clean Drinking Water & Health Mission

Recognizing that groundwater in semi-arid areas often carries high dissolved solids and fluorides, ADEEP established 4 automated reverse osmosis water kiosks in villages surrounding Kolhar Industrial Area. Each station purifies over 2,000 liters per hour, ensuring zero waterborne illnesses in over 1,500 rural households.

TRANSPARENCY & GOVERNANCE

CSR Governance & Policy Alignment

Our CSR Committee ensures transparent fund allocation, verifiable ground execution, and statutory compliance with Indian Ministry of Corporate Affairs regulations.

Corporate CSR Charter & Allocation

ADEEP GROUP allocates a minimum of 2% of average net profits toward verifiable community development projects. All initiatives undergo baseline needs assessments and post-implementation impact auditing by third-party evaluators.

Governing Statute: Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013
Key Focus Corridors: Bidar (Karnataka) & Kadapa (Andhra Pradesh)
Reporting & Audits: Annual CSR Report filed with statutory auditors
