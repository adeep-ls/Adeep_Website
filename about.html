OUR JOURNEY

17+ Years of Milestones

From a single manufacturing unit in Bidar to a multi-site pharmaceutical powerhouse spanning Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana.

The journey begins in Bidar, Karnataka with a clear vision to manufacture high-quality APIs domestically.

2008
Foundation

Adeep Life Sciences Pvt Ltd Founded

Established at Kolhar Industrial Area, Bidar. First commercial API manufacturing operations launched under WHO-GMP standards.

Commercial Scale

Mirtazapine — Flagship Commercial API

Achieved multi-ton commercial scale for Mirtazapine, establishing ADEEP as a preferred domestic supplier to India's top pharma companies.

2012

First ₹50 Cr+ revenue milestone crossed on the back of Mirtazapine commercial ramp-up.

ISO certifications established the quality management framework across all operations.

2015
Quality Milestone

ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001 Triple Certification

Triple ISO certification achieved — Quality, Environmental, and Occupational Health & Safety management systems fully operational.

Expansion

Adeep Laboratories Pvt Ltd — Unit 3 Launched

Subsidiary entity established at YSRJ Mega Hub, Kopparthi, Kadapa, dedicated to complex intermediates and chiral chemistry.

2018

Strategic entry into Andhra Pradesh's pharmaceutical corridor with the Kopparthi expansion.

Green enzyme-catalyzed routes deployed commercially for Brivaracetam.

2020
Innovation

Green Biocatalysis Centre Established

Advanced enzymatic synthesis routes deployed commercially, reducing hazardous solvents by 40% and achieving >99.8% chiral purity for Brivaracetam intermediates.

Regulatory

USFDA Accredited Plant Acquired — Hyderabad

Strategic acquisition of a USFDA-accredited intermediate manufacturing facility in Hyderabad, securing immediate access to tier-1 regulated markets.

2023

A transformational milestone — direct gateway to the global regulated pharmaceutical market.

₹110 Cr+ group turnover, 250+ employees, 4 plants across 3 states.

2026
Present Day

Unit 2 Mega Hub Under Construction

Mega-scale greenfield expansion at Kopparthi Kadapa underway. Targeting 5x capacity growth with PLI scheme support and USFDA-compliant mega plant.
