A Legacy Built on
Chemistry & Purpose

For 17+ years, ADEEP GROUP has been at the forefront of India's pharmaceutical manufacturing revolution — transforming complex chemistry into high-purity commercial APIs and advanced intermediates for global pharmaceutical markets.

WHO WE ARE

Driven by Science. Guided by Values.

ADEEP GROUP encompasses Adeep Life Sciences Pvt Ltd and Adeep Laboratories Pvt Ltd — two integrated entities united by a singular commitment to pharmaceutical excellence.

🎯

Our Mission

To be India's most trusted API and advanced intermediate manufacturer — delivering world-class pharmaceutical chemistry that eliminates critical import dependencies and empowers the global generic drug industry.

🔭

Our Vision

To establish ADEEP as a globally recognized, world-class API powerhouse by 2028 — leveraging green biocatalysis, enzymatic innovation, and full vertical integration across the pharmaceutical value chain.

⭐

Our Philosophy

We believe great chemistry drives pharmaceutical progress. Every molecule we manufacture represents an uncompromising commitment to purity, regulatory compliance, and consistent commercial supply for global pharmaceutical formulators. Quality is non-negotiable.

OUR JOURNEY

17+ Years of Milestones

From a single manufacturing unit in Bidar to a multi-site pharmaceutical powerhouse spanning Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana.

The journey begins in Bidar, Karnataka with a clear vision to manufacture high-quality APIs domestically.

2008
Foundation

Adeep Life Sciences Pvt Ltd Founded

Established at Kolhar Industrial Area, Bidar. First commercial API manufacturing operations launched under WHO-GMP standards.

Quality Milestone

ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001 Triple Certification

Triple ISO certification achieved — Quality, Environmental, and Occupational Health & Safety management systems fully operational.

2015

ISO certifications established the integrated quality and EHS management framework across all operations.

Establishing ADEEP's central innovation, route design, and advanced analytical research powerhouse.

2021
R&D Center

ADEEP Technology Center Established — Hyderabad

Dedicated Central R&D facility operationalized in Hyderabad, driving innovative route scouting, process chemistry, green biocatalysis, and ICH-grade analytical research.

Expansion

Adeep Laboratories Pvt Ltd — Unit 3

Specialized subsidiary entity established at APIIC YSRJ Mega Hub, Kopparthi, Kadapa, dedicated to complex heterocyclic intermediates and chiral building blocks.

2024

Strategic entry into Andhra Pradesh's pharmaceutical corridor with the Kopparthi intermediates hub.

Mega-scale capacity expansion underway to power global multi-ton pharmaceutical supply.

2024
Mega Expansion

Unit 2 Mega Hub Under Construction

Mega-scale greenfield expansion underway at APIIC Plot 27A, YSRJ Mega Hub, Kopparthi, Kadapa. Adding 1000+ KL upcoming reactor capacity under PLI scheme support.

Regulatory Milestone

Unit 1 (Bidar) WHO-GMP Approved

Flagship commercial API manufacturing facility at Kolhar Industrial Area, Bidar successfully receives prestigious WHO-GMP certification, validating world-class cGMP standards.

2025

Prestigious WHO-GMP regulatory approval validates highest standards of quality, safety, and cGMP compliance.
