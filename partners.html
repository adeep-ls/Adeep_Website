PARTNERSHIP BENEFITS

What You Get as an ADEEP Partner

Our partners receive far more than API supply — they gain a complete technical, regulatory, and commercial support ecosystem.

01 / SUPPLY SECURITY

Guaranteed Supply Continuity

Multi-site manufacturing network ensures uninterrupted API supply even during plant maintenance, audits, or scale-up transitions.

02 / REGULATORY

Full DMF & Dossier Support

Complete Drug Master Files, CoAs, Route of Synthesis, ICH stability data, and impurity profiles for seamless regulatory submissions globally.

03 / TECHNICAL

Dedicated Technical Account

A dedicated technical account manager with PhD-level chemistry expertise to support formulation development and process troubleshooting.

04 / COMMERCIAL

Competitive Long-Term Pricing

Backward-integrated supply chain ensures consistently competitive pricing with multi-year price stability guarantees for strategic partners.

05 / CUSTOM

Custom Synthesis & CDM

Contract development and manufacturing (CDM) available for novel APIs, scale-up projects, and import-substitution molecules under full NDA confidentiality.

06 / SAMPLE

Priority Sample Program

High-purity evaluation samples dispatched within 48 hours for qualifying pharma partners — enabling rapid supplier qualification and formulation studies.
