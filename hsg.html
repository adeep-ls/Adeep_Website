OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY

Industrial Safety & Process Hazard Management

Engineered safety barriers designed to safeguard our workforce, plant machinery, and surrounding industrial corridors.

🛡️

Process Safety & HAZOP Studies

Every commercial chemical reaction undergoes comprehensive differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) and runaway modeling before transfer to commercial reactor trains.

  • Automated emergency nitrogen blanketing across all 350+ KL reactors.
  • Rupture discs and dual safety relief valves inspected and calibrated quarterly.
  • Flameproof (Ex-d) electrical classifications across all bulk solvent zones.
🚒

Perimeter Fire Ring & ERT

An automated industrial fire ring network maintains continuous 8.0 kg/cm² pressure with dedicated diesel pumps and high-velocity water sprays.

  • Dedicated trained Emergency Response Team (ERT) on all shifts.
  • Quarterly unannounced plant evacuation and chemical spill response drills.
  • Real-time automated flammable gas leak sensors linked to central alarms.
🩺

Occupational Health & Hygiene

Proactive monitoring of employee well-being through annual medical evaluations, biological monitoring, and continuous solvent vapor threshold surveillance.

  • Personal air sampling monitors worn to verify solvent threshold limit values (TLVs).
  • Full-time occupational health nurse and round-the-clock medical room on campus.
  • Comprehensive Level-A, B, and C PPE tailored to specific chemical handling zones.
