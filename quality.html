ANALYTICAL CAPABILITIES

In-House QC Testing Methods

Comprehensive analytical testing performed throughout every stage of production — from raw material qualification to final product release.

HPLC / UPLC
High-Performance Liquid Chromatography for assay, related substances, and chiral purity determination. Validated per ICH Q2.
GC / HS-GC
Gas Chromatography for residual solvents (ICH Q3C), organic volatile impurities, and reaction byproduct profiling.
UV-Vis Spectroscopy
Identification, quantification, and color testing of raw materials and intermediates per pharmacopoeial specifications.
Karl Fischer Titration
Precise water content determination for moisture-sensitive APIs and hygroscopic intermediates.
Microbial Testing
Total aerobic microbial count (TAMC), TYMC, and specified pathogen testing per USP/EP <61><62>.
Particle Size Analysis
Laser diffraction (d90, d50, d10) for finished API powders critical to downstream formulation performance.
Polarimetry
Optical rotation measurement for chiral API confirmation and enantiomeric purity verification.
