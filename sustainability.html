🛡️

Process Safety & HAZOP

Every commercial chemical process undergoes rigorous Hazard and Operability (HAZOP) studies, differential scanning calorimetry (DSC), and reaction runaway hazard modeling before plant loading.

  • Automated emergency nitrogen blanketing across all reactors
  • Rupture discs and safety relief valves calibrated quarterly
  • Flameproof (Ex-d) electrical classification across all solvent areas
🚒

Fire Safety & Emergency Readiness

Our dedicated fire hydrant ring network with automatic diesel backup pumps maintains 8.0 kg/cm² pressure across the entire industrial perimeter. Medium-velocity water spray systems guard bulk solvent tanks.

  • On-site trained Emergency Response Team (ERT) on all shifts
  • Monthly unannounced evacuation and fire drills
  • Automated flammable gas leak detectors tied to central alarms
🩺

Occupational Hygiene & Health

Comprehensive pre-employment and annual health checkups for 100% of personnel, including pulmonary function tests, liver function tests, audiometry, and continuous workplace solvent vapor exposure monitoring.

  • Personal air sampling monitors for workplace solvent threshold limits
  • Top-grade personal protective equipment (PPE) provided to all
  • Full-time occupational health nurse and on-campus clinic
